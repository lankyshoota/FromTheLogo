How to play

Seven NBA prompts, same for everyone today. Name a player who fits each one. The fewer people who pick your guy, the rarer the card you pull and the more it's worth. LeBron fits a lot of prompts. He's also worth almost nothing.

Every point moves your shooter one foot further from the rim. A 100-point pull sends him back 100 feet.

One prompt at a time with a 20-second clock. Three tries per prompt; run out of time or tries and it's a brick. Each player can only be used once per pack. Franchise history counts: Sonics = Thunder, Bullets = Wizards, Vancouver = Grizzlies, New Jersey = Nets.
